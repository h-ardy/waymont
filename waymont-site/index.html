What we
believe

Four ideas behind every site we make, and what each one means for you.

A website shouldn't break the bank

A good site can cost thousands. For a bakery, a detail shop or a school club, that money is better spent on the business.

So our prices are low and fixed. A starter site is $350. You know the number before we start, and it doesn't change halfway through.

Taste is a human job

We use AI tools to write code faster. We don't let them decide what your business looks like.

A person picks the fonts, colors, photos and words, and says no when something looks like every other site out there.

Plain words, start to finish

You shouldn't need a dictionary to hire a web designer.

We explain what we're doing in normal language. Ask what something costs or how long it takes, and you get a number.

You own what we build

Your site is set up under your name. If you ever stop working with us, it stays with you.

We show you how to make small changes yourself. If you'd rather not, our monthly help covers them.

Every client gets a site that looks like their business, not a template.
